Exploring coffee consumer insights

I wanted to spend more time working on consumer insights, so I put together a project around coffee. It is a familiar product, but there are plenty of questions behind what people buy, how they prepare it, and what matters to them when choosing it.

Why coffee?

Coffee gave me a way to explore consumer packaged goods, or CPG, through something people buy regularly. Packaged coffee is part of that category. I was interested in how consumer research could help explain the choices people make and what those choices might mean for a brand.

What I wanted to practise

My goal was to build a report that connects analysis with questions about consumers. I wanted to practise explaining what the data shows, why a finding might matter, and what would need more research. The charts are one part of that. The explanation needs to give someone a reason to care about the numbers.

Looking at the results carefully

A question I wanted to keep in mind throughout the project was how far the findings could reasonably go. Who answered a survey matters, and a pattern in one dataset does not necessarily describe the whole coffee market. I wanted to be careful about separating what the data supports from what might be worth exploring next.

What comes next

I want to keep building my experience in consumer insights and market research. This project gives me something to build on as I learn more about research methods, reporting, and how brands use consumer feedback to make decisions.
