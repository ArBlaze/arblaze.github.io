Why I redesigned my portfolio website

I wanted to try out GPT Astra, and rebuilding my portfolio seemed like a good way to test it. My old website worked, but I wanted something simpler, easier to navigate, and more modern.

What I wanted to change

I wanted to keep the main sections in one HTML file so visitors could move between my projects, writing, and resume without jumping between pages. I also wanted to clean up the folders and remove files I no longer needed. Individual posts and projects still have their own pages, but the main site is easier to keep track of now.

What I learned

This was a curious quest in seeing a simplest capabilities of AI. It is quite remarkable how far things have come. Now that the setup is simpler, I’m hoping to spend more time sharing projects and writing about what I’m learning. I will say, the generation process was not without faults. Certain details were hallcuinated, such as the interesting number of years of the places I worked at, so it's always good to continue reviewing for any errors it has. AI is not, and probably won't be perfect for a long time.
